Run game night from your phone.

Toronto Poker Club is a small, private app for our home poker league. It runs the tournament clock, records who finished where, and keeps the season standings up to date for everyone in the group.

⏱️

Tournament timer

A shared blinds clock that stays in sync across everyone’s phones, with a Lock Screen Live Activity, a widget, and Chromecast support for the TV.

🏆

Results & standings

Record eliminations and placements as the night goes, and see the season leaderboard, bonus chips, and Final Game™ picture update automatically.

🔔

Notifications

Optional push notifications when a game starts, blinds go up, or someone busts out.

😈

A little trash talk

Optional AI-generated roasts for the winner and anyone who gets knocked out.

Who it’s for

This is not a gambling service or a public poker app. No money is handled in the app, and there’s no online play. It’s only for members of the Toronto Poker Club league, who get the club passcode from the organizer.
