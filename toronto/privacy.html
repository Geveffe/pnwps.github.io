Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Overview

Toronto Poker Club (“the app”) is a private app used by members of a home poker league to run a tournament timer and track game results and season standings. There are no user accounts. Access is protected by a club passcode shared by the organizer.

2. Information the app stores

  • League data: player names (as entered by league members), game dates, buy-ins, eliminations, placements, chip and bonus totals, timer state, and any notes members add about games or players.
  • Notification tokens: if you allow notifications, a push token for your device is stored so the app can send game alerts and update the Lock Screen Live Activity.

The app does not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, payment information, or advertising identifiers, and it does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. How information is used

  • To show the timer, results, and standings to league members.
  • To send game notifications you’ve opted into.
  • To generate optional humorous “roasts” when a player wins or is eliminated (see below).

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) stores league data in Canada and delivers notifications.
  • Apple Push Notification service delivers notifications and Live Activity updates to iOS devices.
  • Anthropic generates roasts. When one is requested, the player’s name, placement, game details, and any notes about that player are sent to Anthropic’s API to write the message. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Google Cast: if you cast the timer to a TV, the app uses your local network to find Chromecast devices. This stays on your local network.

5. Data retention and deletion

League history is kept for as long as the league keeps using the app. To have your name or notes removed from past games or seasons, contact the organizer at the email below and it will be removed.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Access requires the club passcode, but no system is 100% secure, so please don’t put sensitive personal information in player names or notes.

7. Children

The app is meant for adult members of a private poker league and is not directed to children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change too.
