Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Owe Boy (“the app”) is an iOS app that helps groups of friends track shared expenses and figure out who owes whom. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it’s used, and who can see it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your display name and email address, provided when you create an account. Your password is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication (Google) — Owe Boy never sees or stores your raw or hashed password itself.
  • Group and expense data: group names, group membership, expense descriptions, amounts, dates, who paid, who owes, and any payments you record between group members.

Owe Boy does not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any advertising identifiers, and does not include any analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To show you the groups, expenses, and balances you’re part of.
  • To calculate who owes whom, and by how much, within a group.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing.

4. Who can see your information

Your display name and email are visible to other members of any group you belong to — this is necessary so people can add you to groups and see who they’re splitting expenses with. Expense and payment details for a group are only visible to that group’s members, enforced server-side, not just in the app itself.

5. Real money

Owe Boy does not move money. It only keeps a record of who paid for what and who owes whom. Any “payment” recorded in the app is simply a note that a real-world payment happened somewhere else (cash, a bank transfer, another app, etc.) — Owe Boy has no access to your bank account, card, or any payment processor.

6. Data storage and security

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) over encrypted connections. Access to a group’s data is restricted to that group’s members by server-side security rules. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your information is retained for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the email below — we’ll remove your account information and any expense or payment data tied to it.

8. Children’s privacy

Owe Boy is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect information from, children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
