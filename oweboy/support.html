Getting started

Create an account with a display name, email, and password. You can save your password to your device’s Keychain so you don’t have to type it again next time.

How groups work

Create a group and add members from the people who already have accounts. Inside a group, you can rename it or add and remove members at any time.

Adding an expense

In a group, tap the “+” on the Expenses tab. Enter a description and amount, choose who paid (one person, or split across several), and choose who owes — everyone in the group, or just specific people. Expenses can optionally have a date; clear it if you don’t want one, and it’ll show up under “Unspecified Date” in the list.

How balances work

The Balances tab shows, for each person, how much they’re owed or how much they owe in total — tap a name to see the individual amounts behind that total. Owe Boy automatically simplifies debts across the whole group, so you might be asked to pay someone you never directly split an expense with, to settle everyone up in as few payments as possible.

Recording a payment

When money actually changes hands (cash, a bank transfer, whatever you use), tap the “+” on the Balances tab to record it. You can record a payment between any two people in the group — not just the ones a suggested balance names — and everyone’s totals will adjust correctly. Owe Boy doesn’t move real money itself; it just keeps the record straight.

Trouble signing in

Double-check your email and password, and make sure Caps Lock isn’t on. If you still can’t get in, email us and we’ll help sort it out.

Deleting your account

Email us and we’ll delete your account and its associated data. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we store.
